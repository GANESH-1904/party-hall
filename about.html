It started with a shared table

Our story

In 2009, the Narayanan family, three generations of caterers, opened a single hall with forty tables. Guests came for the food and stayed for the welcome.

Today Aurelia hosts over a thousand celebrations, yet every event begins the same way: a conversation about what you want to feel.

About us – FAQ

A little more about how we work.

Is Aurelia family-owned?

Yes. The Narayanan family still runs the venue and the kitchen.

Do you host non-vegetarian events?

Yes. We offer vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus with separate kitchens.

Are you open on public holidays?

We host events on most holidays; our office hours follow the posted schedule.
