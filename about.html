A few quick answers

Good to know

How do I hold a date?

Choose a hall and date in your dashboard and pay the first installment (40%).

Can I bring my own caterer?

Yes. Select “No catering” in the add-ons and the hall price applies.

Can I cancel a booking?

You can cancel from your dashboard, and the date is released immediately.

Is parking available?

Free parking is included, with valet service on the Platinum package.
