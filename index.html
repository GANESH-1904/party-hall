Good to know before you book

Questions, answered

Quick answers from our events desk.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekend wedding dates fill 8–10 months ahead. Birthdays and corporate events can often be arranged within 4–6 weeks.

Can I bring an outside caterer?

Our in-house kitchen is our signature, but we welcome specialty vendors for select cuisines with prior approval.

How do installments work?

Pay a booking amount to hold the date, then follow the schedule in your client dashboard until the final balance is due.

Do you offer a venue visit?

Yes. Book a visit and a planner will walk you through the halls, menus and decor samples.
