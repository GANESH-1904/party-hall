Package questions

Good to know

Answers to the most common questions.

Are taxes included?

Prices exclude GST, which is added to the final quote.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade up to 30 days before the event, subject to availability.

What is the cancellation policy?

The booking amount is refundable up to 90 days before the event, minus a processing fee.

Is there a payment gateway?

Online payments plug into Stripe or PayPal buttons in your dashboard.
